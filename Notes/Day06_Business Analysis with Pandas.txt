## Day 06 – Business Analysis with Pandas (using python)

## Objective
Analyze business performance by answering real business questions using Pandas

## Key Findings

### Sales by Category

- Office Supplies generated the highest sales.
- Furniture ranked second.
- Technology ranked third.

### Profit by Category

- Office Supplies achieved the highest profit.
- Technology generated more profit than Furniture despite lower sales.

### Top Sub-Categories

- Binders
- Paper
- Furnishings
- Phones
- Accessories

### Sales by Region

- West recorded the highest sales.
- South had the lowest sales performance.


### Sales by Customer Segment

- Consumer is the largest customer segment.
- Corporate ranked second.
- Home Office generated the lowest sales.

---

## Skills Learned

- Using `groupby()` for business analysis.
- Aggregating data with `sum()`.
- Sorting results using `sort_values()`.
- Extracting business insights from aggregated data.
- Comparing business performance across different dimensions.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Office Supplies is the strongest-performing category in both sales and profit. 
The West region contributes the highest sales, while the Consumer segment generates the largest share of revenue.
These findings provide valuable insights for future business decisions and marketing strategies.
