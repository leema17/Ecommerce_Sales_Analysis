#  Day 08 - Time Series Analysis (using Python)

##  Objective

Analyze sales and profit trends over time to understand business performance and identify seasonal patterns.

## Key Findings

### Sales by Year

- Sales increased consistently from 2014 to 2017.
- 2017 recorded the highest annual sales.

### Profit by Year

- Profit increased every year.
- 2017 achieved the highest annual profit.

### Sales by Month

- November and December generated the highest sales.
- January and February recorded the lowest sales.
- Sales appear to increase during the last quarter of the year, suggesting possible seasonal demand.

---

## Skills Learned

- Working with datetime columns using Pandas.
- Extracting Year and Month from dates.
- Grouping time-based data with `groupby()`.
- Creating line charts and bar charts using Matplotlib.
- Identifying business growth trends and seasonal patterns.


---

## Conclusion

The time series analysis indicates steady business growth throughout the four-year period. 
Sales and profits increased consistently, with strong performance during the final months of the year.
These insights can help support forecasting and future business planning.
