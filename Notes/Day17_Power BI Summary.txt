Day 17 — Power BI Summary (using Power BI)

Today I started building my Power BI dashboard using the Superstore dataset. 
I imported the data, verified the data types, created key DAX measures, and built interactive visualizations to analyze sales and profit performance.

- Imported the Superstore dataset into Power BI.
- Checked and verified the correct data types for the columns.
- Created DAX measures for:
1) Total Sales
2) Total Profit
3) Total Orders
4) Total Customers
5) Profit Margin
- Learned how to use SUM() and DISTINCTCOUNT() in DAX.
- Learned how to use DIVIDE() safely when calculating ratios.
- Created KPI Cards to display the main business metrics.
-Created visualizations for:
1) Sales & Profit by Category
2) Sales by Region
3) Sales by Segment
4) Top 10 Customers by Sales
5) Top 10 Products by Sales
6) Profit by Category
7) Profit by Region
8) Sales & Profit Trend by Year
9) Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
10) Added interactive Slicers for:
1* Year
2* Region
3* Category
- Learned how slicers interact with other visuals and dynamically update the dashboard.
- Learned that a Power BI project can have multiple pages instead of putting every visualization on one page.
- Started organizing the dashboard into an Executive Overview and future detailed analysis pages.

Outcome:

Today I learned how to build an interactive Power BI dashboard from a cleaned dataset. 
I practiced creating DAX measures, KPI cards, charts, filters, and slicers to analyze sales, profit, customers, products, categories, regions, and trends. 
I also learned how to organize multiple visualizations across different dashboard pages to create a clear and professional Data Analyst portfolio project.
